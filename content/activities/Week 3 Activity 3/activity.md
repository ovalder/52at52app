# Story (EN)

Instead of another 15 km to finish the week's goal, I asked Google Health's AI assistant for the best strategy, since this Saturday I'm running a 10 km event here in Aracaju. Its recommendation: run only 7.5 km, then another 7.5 km the next day, keeping my heart rate in zone 2.

I went for it. I kept the pace slow, as recommended, and ended up running a bit more than planned, but still under 10 km. That leaves only 5 km for tomorrow. All good.

I also ran at an unusual time for me, starting around 6:30 p.m. That's part of the preparation too, since Saturday's event starts at 7 p.m.
