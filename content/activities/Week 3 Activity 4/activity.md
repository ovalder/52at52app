# Story (EN)

This was the last piece of Week 3, and a short one. The goal was 5 km. I ran a bit more, but it was still a very short, easy run, to continue preparing for Saturday's event: the Energisa Electric Run, 10 km.

The event apparently will have much bigger infrastructure than I expected. I ran past the starting location today, and there's a huge stage with speakers, tents that are probably for sponsors, and a giant starting line sign with colored lights and a rotating light cannon. For a maximum distance of 10 km? I really didn't expect that.

It's a shame I didn't think to take any pictures today. I'll do it tomorrow.

Still, a run post needs a photo, even if it's fake. So I asked Gemini to create a totally fake picture of me running. Any resemblance to an actual photo from today's run is purely coincidental, because there isn't one.

The numbers: 5.67 km at 6:28/km, moving time 36:40, no audiobook. With this run, Week 3 closed at 52.89 km, all on foot.
