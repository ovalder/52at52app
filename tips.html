📱 Install this site as an app

Open the shareable installation guide

This website works like an app on your phone: it gets its own icon, opens full-screen, and even keeps working offline for the pages you already visited.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open this site in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow)
  3. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”

Android

  1. Open this site in Chrome
  2. Tap the menu (⋮)
  3. Tap “Install app” (or “Add to Home screen”)

On a computer, look for the install icon (⊕) in the address bar of Chrome or Edge.
